# The 52 keys: what each reads, and when it can be called

Written by `calls.py` from the rules in `build.py`. The same list, with every field, is in `data/key_registry.json`.

A key is **unresolved** until the figures or the event its rule needs exist; **provisional** once every figure is published and the rule has been applied, while the figures can still be revised; **settled** when it is called on evidence that will not change; **held** when the evidence should be there and is not, or two sources disagree. A missing figure never counts as no.

The count is closed on **August 1, 2028**, on the figures published by then. That day is a proposal: the model says only that June figures are used and that a forecast made at the end of July can use them.

## What is still done by hand, and what would end it

**D01, D02, D03, D05, D06.** One line in data/congress_seats.csv when a new Congress meets, every two years. The 2026 result is entered. *What would end it:* Could be read from the public list of sitting members (github.com/unitedstates/congress-legislators). Not needed again before 2031.

**E01.** Entered once: the president cannot run again. *What would end it:* None needed.

**E03.** Entered by hand in data/outlook.json after the June 2028 polls. No list of polls naming a third candidate has been approved as the source, and the rule does not say how to average them. *What would end it:* Read the national polls that name a third candidate from the poll list The Margin already uses, average by pollster as approval is averaged, and call the key on that. Needs your approval of the list and the average.

**E04.** Entered by hand in data/outlook.json. Delegate counts are published by the parties and by news organisations, not as an open feed, and the rule does not say which count is the evidence. *What would end it:* Decide the evidence: the pledged count when the primaries end in June (known before the count is closed), or the convention roll call (which may come after it). Then one entry, with its source.

**E05, E06, E07.** One line in data/nh_primaries.csv once both nominees are known. New Hampshire publishes its results as documents, not as a feed, and "the nominee" is not official until the convention. *What would end it:* Decide who counts as the nominee before the conventions (for example, a majority of pledged delegates). Then the line can be entered the day that happens, with the Secretary of State as its source.
